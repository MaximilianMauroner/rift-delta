How to read this sample

Manual scoresEvery score is assigned by hand. No formula, model, or confidence interval. A low score describes this sample, not a cause of a loss.

A fairer modelCompare within role, account for champion, patch, and opponent, weight Worlds above regular season, and show uncertainty and coverage.

Proposed sourcesOracle's Elixir for stats. Leaguepedia or official LoL Esports for rosters and results. Nothing was fetched.

Gold at 15 is the lane gold difference at 15 minutes. Damage and vision are per minute. All values are fictional.
