Sources and method

All fields are illustrative. The impact and team scores are manually assigned and have no formula. A lower score does not mean a player caused a loss.

Future sources could include Oracle's Elixir for game and player stats, and Leaguepedia or official LoL Esports records for rosters and results. None were fetched for this concept.

For production, compare players in the same role, account for champion, patch and opponent strength, and give Worlds games more weight than regular-season games when events are pooled. Show uncertainty and data coverage.
