How to read this sample

Scores

  • Sample impact score, 0 to 100. Higher is better.
  • Player and team scores are set by hand for this design. No formula computes them.
  • Best on team and lowest observed compare one team. Sample MVP is the top score of all 10 players.
  • A low score shows weaker numbers. It does not show who caused a loss.

Proposed for the real report

  • Compare each player only with players in the same role.
  • Account for champion, patch and opponent strength.
  • Weight Worlds games more than regular season games when events are pooled.
  • Show uncertainty and game coverage next to every score.
  • Use Oracle's Elixir for game and player stats. Use Leaguepedia or official LoL Esports records for rosters and results.
